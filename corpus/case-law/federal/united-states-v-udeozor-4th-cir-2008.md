# United States v. Udeozor, 515 F.3d 260 (4th Cir. 2008)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — precedente de corte federal de apelaciones (4th Cir.), *Published*.

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** el *Appendix: Case Law References for T Visa Adjudications* (3 USCIS-PM B) lo cita bajo **un** concepto — *threats of harm or serious harm*. Conspiración para mantener en **servidumbre involuntaria** (18 U.S.C. §§ 371, 1584) a una niña de 14 años traída de Nigeria con el pasaporte de otra persona y explotada cinco años sin paga; el abuso sexual sufrido fue una de las formas de fuerza que la mantuvieron en esa condición.

## Holding (verbatim)

> "We are asked to review the rulings of the district court in the trial and sentencing of Dr. Adaobi Stella Udeozor for conspiracy to hold another in involuntary servitude and for harboring a juvenile alien. … We find that these claims lack merit, and we affirm the defendant's conviction and sentence."

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "The victim lived with the Udeozors from October 1996 until October 2001. During that time, the Udeozors required her to care for their children, to clean their house, and to cook for them. The victim testified at trial that she was also required to work in Dr. Udeozor's medical office … The victim received no compensation for her work. … The Udeozors never enrolled the victim in any school.
>
> The Udeozors also emotionally abused the victim. The Udeozors threatened to send the victim back to Nigeria, and they told her that the government would deport her if she left the house because she did not have 'papers.'"

## Alcance / cómo citar

USCIS cita una proposición bajo *threats of harm or serious harm*, en \*265 — el abuso sexual como forma de fuerza que sostiene la servidumbre involuntaria:

> "Specifically, to make its case against Dr. Udeozor, the government had to establish … that Dr. Udeozor was part of a conspiracy to 'compel' the victim 'to work against her will, for the benefit of another person or persons, by the use of force, the threat of force, or the threat of legal coercion.' Mr. Udeozor's sexual abuse of the victim was one of the forms of force used to keep the minor victim in the condition of involuntary servitude."
