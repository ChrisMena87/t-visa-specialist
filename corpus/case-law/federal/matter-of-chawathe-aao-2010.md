# Matter of Chawathe, 25 I&N Dec. 369 (AAO 2010)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — precedente de case law transversal (**AAO precedent designado**, Interim Decision #3700). *(Ver Nota de nivel — es otra especie dentro del Nivel 4.)*

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** uno de los 7 casos del README de la KB (frente **SEPARADO** del apéndice del PM). El README lo etiqueta "burden of proof (preponderance)"; con precisión, Chawathe fija el **estándar de prueba** aplicable a *todos* los procedimientos administrativos de inmigración (incluido el T-visa): preponderancia de la evidencia. *(El holding primario del caso es otro —cuándo una corporación de capital abierto es "American firm or corporation" bajo INA § 316(b) para naturalización—; no es por eso que se referencia. Ver Regla 1: holding ≠ pasaje citado.)*

## Holding (verbatim)

> "(3) In most administrative immigration proceedings, the applicant must prove by a preponderance of evidence that he or she is eligible for the benefit sought.
> (4) Even if the director has some doubt as to the truth, if the petitioner submits relevant, probative, and credible evidence that leads the director to believe that the claim is 'more likely than not' or 'probably' true, the applicant has satisfied the standard of proof."

*(Holding primario del caso —headnotes (1)-(2)—: una corporación de capital abierto puede considerarse "American firm or corporation" bajo INA § 316(b) si está incorporada en EE. UU. y cotiza exclusivamente en mercados bursátiles de EE. UU. Disposición: "ORDER: The appeal is sustained.")*

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "The applicant is an employee of ChevronTexaco Corporation who was granted permanent resident status on June 26, 2000, as a member of a profession holding an advanced degree or having exceptional ability. … the applicant filed an Application to Preserve Residence for Naturalization Purposes (Form N-470) … The applicant seeks to preserve his residence for naturalization purposes under section 316(b) of the Act as a lawful permanent resident who will be temporarily absent from the United States for the purpose of employment with an 'American firm or corporation.'"

## Alcance / cómo citar

USCIS/el README lo invocan por el **estándar de preponderancia** (§ III, \*375-376). Verbatim del tribunal:

> "Except where a different standard is specified by law, a petitioner or applicant in administrative immigration proceedings must prove by a preponderance of evidence that he or she is eligible for the benefit sought. … The 'preponderance of the evidence' standard requires that the evidence demonstrate that the applicant's claim is 'probably true' … Even if the director has some doubt as to the truth, if the petitioner submits relevant, probative, and credible evidence that leads the director to believe that the claim is 'more likely than not' or 'probably' true, the applicant or petitioner has satisfied the standard of proof."

**Precisión (estándar vs. burden) — el propio fallo la marca (nota 7, \*375):**

> "The standard of proof should not be confused with the burden of proof. The burden of proving eligibility for the benefit sought remains entirely with the applicant."

Es decir: la etiqueta del README ("burden of proof (preponderance)") comprime dos cosas que Chawathe separa — el **burden** (siempre del solicitante) y el **standard** (preponderancia). La proposición citable es el **estándar**.
