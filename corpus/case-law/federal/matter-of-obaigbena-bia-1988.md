# Matter of Obaigbena, 19 I&N Dec. 533 (BIA 1988)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — precedente de case law transversal (**BIA precedent designado**, Interim Decision #3042). *(Ver Nota de nivel — el BIA vincula más ancho que la AAO.)*

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** uno de los 7 casos del README de la KB (frente **SEPARADO** del apéndice del PM). El README lo etiqueta "counsel assertions ≠ evidence". La proposición citable es esa regla probatoria: **las declaraciones o aseveraciones del abogado no son evidencia**. En la propia decisión vive en la **nota 2**, no en los headnotes. *(Los holdings primarios —headnotes (1)-(4)— son otros: el peticionario debe tener oportunidad razonable de rebatir la evidencia derogatoria antes de la decisión; las solicitudes razonables y oportunas de prórroga deben tratarse con equidad; qué hace "razonable" a una solicitud de prórroga; y que, si el peticionario no responde a tiempo ni pide prórroga razonable, el BIA no considerará evidencia ofrecida por primera vez en apelación. Ver Regla 1: holding ≠ pasaje citado.)*

## Holding (verbatim)

> "(1) A petitioner must be afforded a reasonable opportunity to rebut the derogatory evidence cited in a notice of intention to deny his visa petition and to present evidence in his behalf before the district director's decision is rendered.
> (2) Reasonable and timely requests for an extension of time to submit a rebuttal to the notice of intention to deny a visa petition should be dealt with by the district director in a reasonable and fair manner …
> (3) To be considered 'reasonable,' a request for an extension of time to submit a rebuttal must state with specificity the reasons for the request and be limited to a finite period …
> (4) Where a petitioner fails to timely and substantively respond to the notice of intention to deny or to make a reasonable request for an extension, the Board of Immigration Appeals will not consider any evidence first proffered on appeal as its review is limited to the record of proceeding before the district director; for further consideration, a new visa petition must be filed."

*(Disposición: "ORDER: The record is remanded to the district director for further proceedings …" — el BIA halló que el district director denegó indebidamente la prórroga y remitió.)*

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "The United States citizen petitioner applied for immediate relative status on behalf of the beneficiary as her spouse under section 201(b) of the Immigration and Nationality Act … In a decision dated October 31, 1985, the district director denied the visa petition. … In a notice dated October 2, 1985, the district director advised the petitioner of his intention to deny the visa petition on the ground that the petitioner had failed to meet her burden of proving the bona fides of her marriage …"

## Alcance / cómo citar

El README lo invoca por la regla **"counsel assertions ≠ evidence"**. La afirmación está en la **nota 2** de la decisión (\*534):

> "We note statements or assertions by counsel are not evidence. Matter of Ramirez-Sanchez, 17 I&N Dec. 503, 506 (BIA 1980)."

Es decir: la etiqueta del README apunta a una **regla probatoria** que Obaigbena enuncia al pasar (nota 2) mientras resuelve la cuestión de debido proceso sobre la prórroga. La proposición citable es que **lo que el abogado asevera, por sí solo, no es prueba** en el record.
