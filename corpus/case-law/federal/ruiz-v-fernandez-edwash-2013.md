# Ruiz v. Fernandez, 949 F. Supp. 2d 1055 (E.D. Wash. 2013)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — case law transversal (tribunal **de distrito**, E.D. Wash.). *(Ver Nota de nivel.)*

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** el *Appendix: Case Law References for T Visa Adjudications* (3 USCIS-PM B) lo cita bajo **un** concepto — *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process*. Acción civil bajo el TVPRA (§ 1595) de tres pastores chilenos con visa H-2A contra el ranchero y la asociación empleadora.

## Holding (verbatim)

> "A jury could reasonably find that these events occurred as described by Plaintiffs, and could infer from these events that Fernandez intended to coerce Plaintiffs while they were employed at his ranch. Moreover, federal courts have held that withholding immigration documents and threatening deportation can serve as the basis for a TVPRA violation. See United States v. Calimlim, 538 F.3d 706, 713 (7th Cir.2008) …"

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "Plaintiffs in this case are three Chilean sheepherders who came to the United States under the H-2A program and worked on Defendant Max Fernandez's ranch. …
>
> Plaintiffs assert that Fernandez required them to perform many tasks other than range sheepherding … including denying them their breaks, threatening to have them deported, not providing them adequate food, and placing them in housing that did not meet the minimum health and safety standards required by the H-2A program. Plaintiffs Ruiz and Castro also assert that Fernandez took their passports from them upon their arrival on the Fernandez ranch and withheld the passports for a period of many months."

## Alcance / cómo citar

USCIS cita este caso bajo *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process*, en \*1076-1077 — retención de pasaportes + amenazas constantes de reporte a inmigración:

> "In support of their TVPRA claim, Plaintiffs have introduced evidence that Fernandez confiscated Plaintiff Ruiz' and Plaintiff Castro's passports when they arrived at the Fernandez ranch, and kept those documents despite Plaintiffs' requests for their return. … All three Plaintiffs testified at their depositions that Fernandez made repeated threats that he would call the police or immigration authorities on them. Plaintiffs characterized these threats as constant and continuous, and believed that Fernandez made these threats to intimidate them."
