# United States v. Veerapol, 312 F.3d 1128 (9th Cir. 2002)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — precedente de corte federal de apelaciones (9th Cir.), *Published*.

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** el *Appendix: Case Law References for T Visa Adjudications* (3 USCIS-PM B) lo cita bajo **un** concepto — *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process*. Condena por **servidumbre involuntaria** (18 U.S.C. § 1584) de una trabajadora tailandesa retenida en la casa y el restaurante de la acusada mediante aislamiento, abuso y amenazas ancladas al estatus migratorio.

## Holding (verbatim)

> "On August 12, 1999, Supawan Veerapol was convicted by a jury of one count of holding another to involuntary servitude in violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1584 … We have jurisdiction pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1291, and we affirm the conviction, sentence, and order of restitution."

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "Veerapol, a native of Thailand and the common-law wife of a Thai ambassador, operated a Thai restaurant in Los Angeles for which she recruited Thai nationals as workers. While in Thailand in the summer of 1989, Veerapol approached Nobi Saeieo, a non-English-speaking Thai villager with a second-grade education, offering her transportation to and two years of employment in the United States at a substantially higher wage than Saeieo could earn in Thailand. … Once through immigration, however, Veerapol reclaimed Saeieo's passport."

## Alcance / cómo citar

USCIS cita una proposición bajo *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process*, en \*1130-31 — control mediante coacción variada, incluida la amenaza de arresto por el estatus migratorio:

> "She maintained control over them through verbal abuse and threats of legal action and physical force. Veerapol refused Saeieo's frequent entreaties to allow her to return to Thailand, at one point telling her that if she left, Veerapol would kill her. … Veerapol also told her that the police in the United States would arrest her as an illegal alien were she to seek their help."
