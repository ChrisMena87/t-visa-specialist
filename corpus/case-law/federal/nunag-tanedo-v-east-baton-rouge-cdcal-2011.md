# Nuñag-Tanedo v. East Baton Rouge Parish School Board, 790 F. Supp. 2d 1134 (C.D. Cal. 2011)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — case law transversal (tribunal **de distrito**, C.D. Cal.). *(Ver Nota de nivel — la tabla del README no subdivide circuit/distrito.)*

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** el *Appendix: Case Law References for T Visa Adjudications* (3 USCIS-PM B) lo cita bajo **un** concepto — *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process*. Acción civil de trafficking bajo el TVPA (§ 1595) por maestros filipinos con visa H-1B reclutados mediante fraude de honorarios; orden que **deniega** la moción de desestimar la pretensión de forced labor.

## Holding (verbatim)

> "The Court GRANTS the Motion as to the predicate acts of mail and wire fraud under Plaintiffs' Racketeer Influenced and Corruption Organization Act ('RICO') claims and DENIES the Motion as to Plaintiffs' remaining claims."

*(Sobre el § 1589(a):)*

> "Based on these factual allegations, including allegations of threats and fraud, Plaintiffs have sufficiently alleged that Defendants violated the TVPA under § 1589(a)."

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "Plaintiffs are teachers and Filipino nationals who came from the Philippines to work in the United States. Plaintiffs were holders of H-1B visas … After Recruiter Defendants had the visas and passports, Recruiter Defendants told Plaintiffs for the first time that Plaintiffs would have to pay a second, larger recruitment fee … Recruiter Defendants told Plaintiffs that if they did not pay the additional fees, Plaintiffs would forfeit fees already paid, would not be permitted to travel to the United States, and would not be given their visas."

## Alcance / cómo citar

USCIS cita este caso bajo *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process*, en \*1144 — las amenazas dirigidas a presionar la continuación del trabajo (aplicando la definición del § 1589(c)(1), "the use or threatened use of a law or legal process … in any manner or for any purpose for which the law was not designed, in order to exert pressure on another person"):

> "Plaintiffs allege multiple threats made by Recruiter Defendants to Plaintiffs to support their claim under § 1589(a)(3). For example, Plaintiffs allege that Recruiter Defendants threatened deportation (1) after certain teachers stated that they planned to move due to high living costs; (2) if a certain teacher tried to bring his family with him to the United States from the Philippines; and (3) after a certain teacher complained to a reporter about poor treatment in the work program. Plaintiffs also allege that Defendant Navarro threatened to sue Plaintiffs for voicing criticisms about Recruiter Defendants' business operations. Finally, Plaintiffs state that Navarro threatened to allow Plaintiffs' visas to expire without renewal and to have Plaintiffs' employment terminated …"
