# Elat v. Ngoubene, 993 F. Supp. 2d 497 (D. Md. 2014)

**Nivel de autoridad:** 4 — case law transversal (tribunal **de distrito**, D. Md.). *(Ver Nota de nivel.)*

**Por qué se referencia (proposición):** el *Appendix: Case Law References for T Visa Adjudications* (3 USCIS-PM B) lo cita bajo **un** concepto — *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process* — **citando dentro a *Camayo v. John Peroulis & Sons Sheep, Inc.*** Acción civil TVPRA (§ 1595) de una trabajadora doméstica camerunesa contra la familia que la trajo.

## Holding (verbatim)

> "Defendants' Motion for Summary Judgment is GRANTED IN PART and DENIED IN PART."

*(Sobre la amenaza de deportación como abuse of legal process — citando a Nunag-Tanedo:)*

> "The threat of deportation 'can constitute serious harm to an immigrant within the meaning of the forced labor statute.' It also '"clearly falls within the concept and definition of 'abuse of legal process'"' when '"the alleged objective for such conduct was to intimidate and coerce [Plaintiff] into forced labor."'"

## Hechos (verbatim del tribunal)

> "In January 2006, while Plaintiff was in her early twenties and living in her homeland of Cameroon, Plaintiff's aunt and uncle … invited her to the United States to live with them and their children … Plaintiff alleges that she did not know that she signed a contract to work as a domestic servant for the Ngoubenes. …
>
> At the Ngoubene home, Plaintiff worked long days 'doing household chores.' Specifically, she prepared breakfast and cooked meals for the family; washed dishes; 'did the guests' laundry'; cleaned the house and shopped for groceries; washed cars; and braided Defendants' hair."

## Alcance / cómo citar

USCIS cita este caso bajo *abuse or threatened abuse of legal process* (\*524-526), **con *Camayo* como cita interna del pasaje** — la amenaza de deportación es abuse of legal process sólo si es un mal uso del proceso, no automáticamente:

> "*Camayo v. John Peroulis & Sons Sheep, Inc.* … is instructive. There, one of the defendants explicitly threatened one of the plaintiffs with deportation twice … The court noted that 'there are other cases in which courts have found that [an] employer's comments about immigration consequences that could occur, do not by themselves constitute an abuse of the legal process; rather, an employee is obligated to show that the threatened use of the legal process is a misuse of that process.' … 'Taken as a whole, this conduct could be found to constitute an abuse of the legal process.'"

> "This is not to say that an employer automatically violates the TVPA by acknowledging the adverse immigration consequences that might befall an employee who is terminated. … Rather, this Court is persuaded that the question of whether statements (and actions) by an employer [constitute an abuse of the legal process] must be viewed in light of all the surrounding circumstances …"

*(Nota — Camayo como cita interna: el apéndice del PM menciona* Camayo v. John Peroulis & Sons Sheep, Inc. *(D. Colo. 2012) DENTRO de la entrada de Elat, no como entrada propia. Se registra aquí, en Alcance, como la fuente que Elat cita para el estándar de "misuse of process" — no se le crea ficha separada salvo que el fundador lo decida.)*
