## Hechos relevantes

Peticionaria italiana de 69 años. Admitida a EE.UU. 2007 como H1B
para trabajar como [redacted] para [redacted] company.

**Esquema laboral según peticionaria:**

- Empleador (el visa petitioner) la forzó a trabajar **sin compensación**
  como caregiver para un disabled individual.
- Cuando preguntaba por pago, decían que su salario estaba siendo
  guardado para "medical and disability benefits" + "lawful permanent
  resident card" que necesitaba para trabajar.
- Incapaz de irse porque:
  - No hablaba inglés
  - Sin dinero
  - No sabía moverse por el neighborhood
  - Temía que si se iba, nunca recibiría pago ni LPR card.

Cuando el disabled individual murió, employer le dio one-way ticket a
Italia, sin pago. Peticionaria demandó a employer por unpaid wages,
llegó a settlement.

Filed T sobre la base de "recruited for labor by fraud and thereafter
subjected to involuntary servitude."

**AAO footnote 2 (importante):**

> "[T]he record does not contain evidence that the Applicant's former
> employer ever used (or threatened use of) physical restraint or
> physical injury, or abused (or threatened abuse of) legal process
> in order to subject the Applicant to a condition of servitude; nor
> does the record show that the Applicant's former employer had a
> 'scheme, plan, or pattern intended to cause [the Applicant] to
> believe' that she or someone else 'would suffer serious harm or
> physical restraint' if she did not enter into or continue in such
> a condition."

(I.e., AAO cuestiona si el trafficking mismo estaba probado, pero no
reaches esa cuestión porque hardship es dispositivo.)

**AAO footnote 3:**

> "The record indicates that the Applicant departed the United States
> in January 2021, and has not returned to date. If this is the case,
> the Applicant might be ineligible for T-1 classification on an
> additional ground, as she would not satisfy the requirement in
> section 101(a)(15)(T)(i)(II) of the Act of being physically present
> in the United States on account of trafficking."

## Razonamiento del AAO sobre hardship

AAO aplicó 8 CFR §214.11(i)(1)(i)-(vii) factor by factor:

**(i) Age + personal circumstances:**
- Strong personal/community ties en US + edad + health issues
- AAO: "may face some difficulties in readjusting to life in Italy
  after the many years she spent in the United States, but the
  evidence does not establish that the related hardship will rise to
  the level of extreme"
- Lived in Italy until age 47, at least one adult child resides en
  Italia → "would not be alone and without support"
- No specific info on Italian work history o social benefits

**(ii) Serious physical/mental illness:**
- Vision loss + knee/back pain + major depressive disorder con anxious
  distress
- Therapy 2015-2017, anti-depressants since 2019
- AAO: "the record does not establish that the necessary treatment
  for her conditions would not be reasonably available in Italy"
- Letter del physician describe vision issues requiring monitoring
  pero no que treatment esté unavailable en Italia
- "[W]hile we do not dispute that separation from the Applicant's
  social network in the United States will cause her some degree of
  emotional and psychological hardship, a finding of extreme hardship
  involving unusual and severe harm may not be based upon disruption
  to social opportunities. 8 C.F.R. § 214.11(i)(1)"

**(iv) Loss of US courts access:**
- Pending civil suit contra ex-attorney por tort of professional
  negligence
- Counsel argues she needs to be in US for deposition, trial
- AAO: "assertions of counsel do not constitute evidence. *Matter of
  Obaigbena*"
- "[T]he record does not show that such difficulties would be
  insurmountable or significantly impair her ability to pursue legal
  action against her former attorney. Nor does the record show that
  the Applicant's case is currently pending jury trial, or that she
  would be unable to participate in any legal proceedings remotely,
  if needed, with assistance of an interpreter."

**(vii) Likelihood trafficker would severely harm:**
- Claims employer "wields enormous power and influence" + associate
  reside en Italia
- AAO: "these statements alone do not establish that the Applicant's
  former employer or other individuals acting on the employer's
  behalf in Italy would be likely to severely harm the Applicant, or
  that the Italian authorities would be unable or unwilling to
  protect her"

## Doctrina destilada

- **Patrón: hardship NO basado en social/community disruption ni en
  age alone.** 8 CFR §214.11(i)(1) explícitamente excluye "current
  or future economic detriment" y "disruption to social or economic
  opportunities". Aún con 14 años en US + strong church community +
  volunteer work + age 69 + health issues, AAO encontró que **no
  rises to extreme**.

  El test mental: ¿qué hardship factor más allá del social/económico
  está bien articulado y probado? Si la respuesta es "ninguno
  sustantivo", el caso fails.

  **Consecuencia para T:** v3.5 §Cobertura — bullet de Extreme hardship,
  agregar caveat sobre que social ties + community involvement + age
  alone son INSUFICIENTES. El skill debe ser estricto al evaluar este
  elemento.

  **Aterriza en:** v3.5 §Cobertura — bullet de hardship.

- **Patrón: medical/psychological hardship requiere mostrar
  UNAVAILABILITY del tratamiento en country of return.** No basta
  tener depresión + anti-depresivos + back pain — la PC debe articular
  que el tratamiento (específico) NO está disponible o NO es
  accesible en el país de retorno.

  Italia tiene sistema de salud universal. La PC NO articuló por qué
  no podría continuar tratamiento ahí. Eso mató el factor.

  **Consecuencia para T:** v3.5 §Preguntas de rescreening — agregar
  pregunta sobre availability of treatment:
  > "Si tiene tratamiento médico/psicológico en US, ¿está disponible
  > el mismo tipo de tratamiento en su país de origen? ¿Sería
  > accesible (cost, geography, language)? Si no, ¿qué evidencia
  > específica puede aportar (reportes country conditions, letters
  > médicos sobre unavailability)?"

  **Aterriza en:** v3.5 §Preguntas de rescreening + v3.5 §Atención del
  abogado.

- **Patrón: retaliation by trafficker requires documented likelihood
  + inability of home country authorities to protect.** Claims
  genéricos de "trafficker tiene power and influence" NO bastan. Se
  requiere:
  - Threat específico documented (testimony, letter, message)
  - Probabilidad razonable de severe harm
  - Inability or unwillingness of home country authorities to provide
    protection

  **Consecuencia para T:** v3.5 §Cobertura — bullet de hardship,
  agregar caveat sobre retaliation: requiere evidencia específica, no
  generalidades.

  **Aterriza en:** v3.5 §Cobertura — hardship.

- **Patrón: counsel's assertions ≠ evidence (REAFIRMADO).** Junto
  con In Re: 13039037, este caso reafirma Matter of Obaigbena. Counsel
  no puede afirmar facts en briefs que la PC no afirma en
  declaraciones.

  **Consecuencia para T:** confirmación de regla 5 (anti-inferencia)
  del v3.4. El skill toma intake al face value y NO infiere facts
  no declarados.

  **Aterriza en:** v3.4 §Reglas — Regla 5 (confirma).

- **Patrón: departure post-T-application puede generar additional
  ground for ineligibility.** AAO footnote 3 — si la PC se va de US
  durante el proceso T, puede caer §214.11(g)(2) (physical presence
  post-departure requires demonstrating continued victimization /
  new trafficking incident / reentry for investigative process).

  **Consecuencia para T:** v3.5 §Atención del abogado — agregar bullet
  permitido:
  > "Si la PC ha viajado fuera de US durante el T process (o piensa
  > viajar), **flag al closer**: explicar §214.11(g)(2) requirements
  > before any departure. Travel during T pendency puede crear ground
  > for denial."

  **Aterriza en:** v3.5 §Atención del abogado.

- **Patrón: labor trafficking by employer with visa control — REFINEMENT
  CRÍTICO.** AAO footnote 2 cuestiona si el trafficking realmente
  estaba probado en este caso. El employer pagando-nada con excusas
  (lo guardamos para GC, etc.) + víctima incapaz de irse por barriers
  prácticas (no English, no money) **NO es automáticamente** TVPA
  trafficking.

  Sin evidencia de:
  - Physical restraint o physical injury (o amenaza de éstos)
  - Threatened abuse of legal process
  - Scheme/plan/pattern intended to cause belief of serious harm
  
  No hay Medios bajo 8 CFR §214.11(a). El sub-pattern "labor trafficking
  by employer with visa control" que extraje de In Re: 11297648 tiene
  que **distinguirse cuidadosamente** de wage theft + barriers to leaving.

  El test refinado para v3.5:
  - Wage theft + víctima atrapada por barriers prácticas (no English,
    no money, no transportation) → probably abuso laboral, NO
    trafficking → flag a U-Visa / DOL claim.
  - Wage theft + threat of physical harm o threat of immigration
    consequences (deportation, cancel visa) o scheme to cause belief
    of serious harm → labor trafficking, ✅ o ⚠️.

  **Consecuencia para T:** v3.5 §Falsos positivos — Abuso laboral,
  agregar caveat fino sobre la distinción:
  > "Wage theft + barriers prácticas to leaving (idioma, dinero,
  > knowledge) **NO es automáticamente labor trafficking**. Se requiere
  > Medios articulados: amenazas de violencia física, threats de
  > inmigración (cancel visa, deport), scheme to cause belief of
  > serious harm. Sin Medios documentados, es wage theft + abuso —
  > U-Visa qualifying si hay crimen, o DOL claim — pero no T."

  **Aterriza en:** v3.5 §Falsos positivos — Abuso laboral (refinement
  del sub-pattern de visa control).
